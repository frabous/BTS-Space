## Beyond The Space 1.1.0

First CurseForge release.

- **SS-03 Carrier Ops** is now a family of strikes delivered by a destroyer. Press **M** again (or ← / → in the H menu) to cycle payloads.
- **SS-03A Asteroid Puller** reworked: the destroyer drops the Graviton Lance AG-07, which harpoons a passing asteroid and drags it onto the target (43 s cinematic).
- **SS-03B Broadside Command**: pilot the destroyer's five twin turrets for 30 seconds.
- **SS-03C Kinetic Rain**: 96 tungsten rods in a four-arm spiral barrage, visible to every player in the dimension.
- 17 new original SS-03 sounds.
- New synthesized sound design for the Railgun, Black Hole, Supernova and asteroid strikes (38 sounds, same timing and mix).

Requires Forge 1.12.2 (14.23.5.2864+) and Java 8, on the server and every client.
